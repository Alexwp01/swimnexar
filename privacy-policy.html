Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nexar Sport LLC, doing business as Nexar Aquatic Academy ("Nexar," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and protects the personal information you provide when registering for our programs at swimnexar.com.

1. Who We Are

Nexar Aquatic Academy is a youth aquatic sports academy located in Pasco and Hillsborough County, Florida, offering Water Polo and Swim Team programs for ages 6–18.

Contact: swimnexar@gmail.com · +1 (838) 333-0666

2. Information We Collect

When you register for a program or free trial, we collect:

  • Parent or guardian name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Child's first name and age
  • Selected program and location (Water Polo — Land O' Lakes or Temple Terrace; Swim Team)
  • Consent to participation and photo/video use (via our Terms & Conditions)

We do not collect payment information directly — payments are processed by third-party providers.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process your registration and schedule your free trial
  • To contact you about your child's training, schedule changes, and updates
  • To organize and manage our programs
  • To send occasional announcements about Nexar events and offerings (you may opt out at any time)

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Who Processes Your Data

Your information may be handled by the following services:

  • Nexar staff — coaches and administrative team for program management
  • Google (Forms & Sheets) — registration data is received and stored via Google Apps Script
  • Meta (Facebook / Instagram) — if you interact with our ads or social pages
  • Vercel — our website hosting provider

All service providers are required to handle your data securely and only for the purposes described above.

5. Data Retention

We retain registration data for the duration of your child's participation in our programs, plus up to 12 months afterward for administrative purposes. You may request deletion at any time (see Section 6).

6. Your Rights — Request Deletion or Access

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications

To exercise any of these rights, email us at swimnexar@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Request". We will respond within 10 business days.

7. Photos and Videos

By agreeing to our Terms & Conditions at registration, you consent to photos and videos of your child taken at Nexar events and practices being used on our website and social media channels (Instagram, Facebook). You may withdraw this consent at any time by emailing swimnexar@gmail.com.

8. Cookies

Our website uses only essential functional cookies (session and security). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

9. Children's Privacy

Our programs are designed for children ages 6–18. We collect information about children only from their parents or legal guardians. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Questions? Contact us at swimnexar@gmail.com or call +1 (838) 333-0666.
